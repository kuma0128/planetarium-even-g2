Explore stars, constellations, the Moon and planets on Even Realities G2. Choose a location and Now, Tonight or a custom date. Adjust heading, elevation, field of view, star brightness, lines and labels; use a full-display or compact sky map. Calculations are local; location is not uploaded.

Enter heading from a compass app or landmark; phone orientation sensors are not used. Tap for Now/Tonight, scroll the temple touchpad or R1 ring left/right by 15 degrees, or double-tap for the exit dialog. Experimental two-pose head tracking follows elevation.

UPDATE LOG: 1.1.0 -> 1.1.1
- Fixed exit cancellation: after selecting No, double-tap opens the exit dialog again. Sky updates resume without reconnecting.
- Pause image transfers while the OS dialog/menu is open; restore controls on dismissal. Added repeated-cancel regression tests.

- Saved language choice locally when storage is available. Selection works without storage; invalid preferences fall back to English.
- Language switching preserves observing location, time and head calibration without restarting the sensor.
- Added localized dates (device time zone), layout adjustments, caption wrapping and text sizing for translated content.

Requires G2 and Even app 2.2.10+. Head-tracking accuracy and optical alignment remain unverified. Sky map, not an AR overlay. Images: simulator captures. Terrain, weather and light pollution are not modeled.
