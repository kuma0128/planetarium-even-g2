Explore the sky from your Even Realities G2. Choose your observing location and a time, then discover stars, constellations, the Moon and planets.

YOUR SKY, YOUR DISPLAY
Enjoy a full-display planetarium with text hidden by default. Switch to a compact map, or enable date and sky information and star/direction labels independently.

PLAN YOUR VIEW
Switch between Now, Tonight and a custom date. Use your current location or enter coordinates. Adjust the elevation, field of view, star brightness limit and constellation lines.

FIND YOUR DIRECTION
Enter the heading you are facing from a compass app or a known landmark; the app does not use your phone's orientation sensors. Choose magnetic or true north. Named objects show their direction and elevation, with a Moon illumination readout.

EXPLORE ON G2
Tap to switch Now / Tonight, scroll the temple touchpad or R1 ring to browse left/right by 15°, and double-tap to open the exit dialog. Experimental head tracking can follow upward and downward movement after two-pose calibration. This feature is still awaiting physical-device validation; sensor support and performance may vary.

LOCAL SKY CALCULATIONS
A bundled catalog includes 2,865 stars, constellation lines, the Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn. Sky calculations run locally. The app does not upload your observing location.

Requires Even Realities G2 and Even app 2.2.10 or later. Choose English (default), Japanese, Korean, or Simplified Chinese in the interface. The display is a sky map, not an optically aligned overlay of real stars. Store images are captured with the Even Hub simulator; actual G2 appearance may differ. Buildings, terrain, weather and light pollution are not modeled.
