G2 Planetarium 1.1.1 — September 15, 2026
Previous submission: 1.1.0

Fixed the September 15 review issue: after double-tapping and choosing No,
the next double-tap can open the exit dialog again without reconnecting.

Changes:
- Corrected OS overlay event handling: event 4 opens the overlay; event 5
  dismisses it. Dismissal restores the app instead of leaving gestures disabled.
- Pause image delivery before requesting the dialog and while an OS overlay
  is open. Dismissal requests a complete current frame; stale transfer results
  cannot close the resumed session.
- System exit and disconnect still stop the app session. Head tracking stops
  while covered and requires calibration again after returning.
- Added regression tests for three consecutive No selections on both startup
  and sky pages, continued taps and frame delivery, and a subsequent Yes exit.

Reviewer check:
1. Open G2 Planetarium. Double-tap, choose No, and double-tap again.
2. Repeat cancellation three times. The dialog should open every time.
3. Set an observing location on the phone and repeat on the sky map.
4. After No, verify tap/scroll controls and sky updates; choose Yes to exit.

Automated checks use the real SDK with a mock native host. Physical G2
verification is not claimed.
